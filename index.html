About Waste2Door

Waste2Door is a Bengaluru-focused doorstep household waste pickup concept designed for residents who are unable to hand over their household waste during regular municipal collection timings.


The idea is to provide a convenient pickup option while ensuring that collected waste is handed over only through an authorised municipal or approved waste-management process.


Waste2Door is currently at the concept and early development stage, with the objective of validating the model through a controlled pilot before scaling.

Our Solution

01

Book Pickup

Customer requests a waste pickup through the Waste2Door platform.

02

Dedicated Rider

A trained rider reaches the customer's doorstep at the selected time.

03

Waste Handover

Segregated household waste is transported to an authorised municipal collection point or approved handover location.

04

Responsible Disposal

Waste enters the authorised municipal waste management system.

How Waste2Door Works

Customer

Books a pickup from home.

Waste2Door Platform

Assigns the pickup request to an available rider.

Rider

Collects segregated waste from the doorstep.

Authorised Handover

Waste is handed over only through an approved municipal or authorised process.

Proposed Pilot Plan

Phase 1 — Regulatory & Operational Validation

Understand applicable municipal requirements and identify authorised waste handover locations and operating procedures.

Phase 2 — Select a Bengaluru Locality

Start with a controlled pilot in one selected locality with a limited number of households and riders.

Phase 3 — Technology & Operations

Develop the customer booking system, rider workflow, payment process and basic operations dashboard.

Phase 4 — Measure & Validate

Track pickup demand, customer satisfaction, operational costs, rider efficiency and repeat usage.

Phase 5 — Scale

If the pilot demonstrates a sustainable and compliant operating model, expand to additional Bengaluru localities.

Government & Regulatory Approach

Waste2Door intends to operate only through an approved and compliant waste-management process.

  • Seek guidance and approval from the relevant municipal authorities.
  • Identify authorised waste collection points or approved handover locations.
  • Follow applicable waste segregation and transportation requirements.
  • Begin with a controlled pilot in a selected Bengaluru locality.
