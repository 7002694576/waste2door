About Waste2Door

Waste2Door is an early-stage concept focused on building a convenient doorstep household waste pickup service for urban households in Bengaluru.


The goal is to combine simple technology, scheduled pickups, customer communication and responsible waste-management practices into one easy-to-use service.

Government & Regulatory Approach

Waste2Door intends to operate in accordance with applicable municipal requirements and relevant solid-waste-management rules.


The operating model will prioritise coordination with authorised municipal bodies, approved waste-management partners and applicable local requirements before commercial-scale operations.
