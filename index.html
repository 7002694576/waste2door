Waste2Door

Doorstep Waste Pickup for Bengaluru

A convenient doorstep household waste pickup service designed for tenants, working professionals and residents who are unable to hand over their waste during regular municipal collection hours.

Partner With Us

The Problem

01

Missed Collection

Many working professionals and tenants are away from home when municipal waste collection vehicles arrive.

02

Storage at Home

Household waste may remain inside homes until the next collection opportunity.

03

Improper Disposal

Lack of convenient disposal options can contribute to waste being placed in unauthorised locations.

Our Solution

01

Book Pickup

Customer requests a waste pickup through the Waste2Door platform.

02

Dedicated Rider

A trained rider reaches the customer's doorstep at the selected time.

03

Waste Handover

Segregated household waste is transported to an authorised municipal collection point or approved handover location.

04

Responsible Disposal

Waste enters the authorised municipal waste management system.

How Waste2Door Works

Customer

Books a pickup from home.

Waste2Door App

Assigns the pickup request to an available rider.

Rider

Collects segregated waste from the doorstep.

Authorised Handover

Waste is handed over only through an approved municipal or authorised process.

Target Customers

Working Professionals

People who leave home before municipal collection and return after collection hours.

Tenants & PG Residents

Residents looking for a convenient and responsible waste disposal option.

Apartments

Apartment residents requiring additional pickup flexibility.

Proposed Business Model

₹100

Proposed starting price per household pickup.

Technology Platform

Customer App

Pickup booking, address, time slot, payment and pickup tracking.

Rider App

Pickup requests, navigation, status updates and proof of collection.

Admin Dashboard

Rider management, customer management, operations, payments and service monitoring.

Government & Regulatory Approach

Waste2Door intends to operate only through an approved and compliant waste-management process.


  • Seek guidance and approval from the relevant municipal authorities.
  • Identify authorised waste collection points or approved handover locations.
  • Follow applicable waste segregation and transportation requirements.
  • Begin with a controlled pilot in a selected Bengaluru locality.
